Snakeboy

I co-developed a pair of custom Snake Camera controllers for Kapture the Snake of '88, a Unity 3D horror game created during the PlayStation Careers Pathway Spring Game Jam. Designed around the concept of a Snake Camera, the controller lets players interact with the game through motion tracking, voice input, and tactile controls. This project is designed by me and Magen Mozeh.

What Are the Key Features?

Game Set Up Diagram

Interactive Features

  • Move: Players can use the joystick to move the environment.
  • Brightness: The potentiometer on the screen is turnable so players adjust the brightness of Snakeboy flashlight.
  • Aim: Rotate the Snakehead to move the on-screen cursor. There's a small LED inside the snake mouth, the LED color changes based on Kaptured Spirits
  • Inspect: The physical controller screen shows ghost signals and key info.
  • Capture: When players see an orb in side their on screen camera, they can press down on the joystick to kapture ghosts.
  • Interviews: After entering the interview stage, players can talk to the microphone for voice-driven conversations with LLM-powered ghosts. For Example, "How was your day?" or "Who do you think killed everyone?" LLM-powered ghosts may lie to deceive players.

Build Process

  • Step 1: Designed the Controller Mechanical Enclosure in Fusion 360 . We first measured the dimensions of all electronic components and then create a multi-part enclosure assembly model in Fusion 360, The current design joints mechanical components using heat-set inserts and 4x M3 screws.
  • Step 2: 3D printed the PLA enclosure, then colored two different color variations using Bambu Studio slicer, and printed it on a Bambu Lab P1S. Did 3x test prints before we finalized the design
  • Step 3: Assembled the raw prototype with breadboards and verified its function.
  • Step 4: Designed, soldered, and integrated the custom PCB and electronics.
  • Step 5: Developed bitmap animations for the OLED display.
  • Step 6: Generated the Bill of Material, progress accessment report, and mechanical drawings.
