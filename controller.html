Custom Controller

A custom Snake camera controller made as a hardware prop for Kapture the Snake of 88', a Unity 3D horror game developed during the PlayStation Careers Pathway Spring Game Jam. Designed around the concept of a Snake Camera, the controller lets players interact with the game through motion tracking, voice input, and tactile controls. This project is designed by me and Magen Mozeh.

Design Showcase

Build Process

  • CAD enclosure design with measured dimensions (Fusion 360)
  • Developed and reviewed custom PCB layouts
  • Created a Bill of Materials (BOM) and component cost-analysis
  • 3D printing (Slicer: Bambu Studio. Printer: Bambu Lab P1s)
  • Raw Prototype fabrication and assembly
  • PCB soldering and electronics integration
  • Testing, debugging, and validation
